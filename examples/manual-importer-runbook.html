Importer Rollback Runbook

Audience: platform on-call engineers. Time to execute: about fifteen minutes.

Prerequisites

kubectl on ops-cluster deploy role in ops on-call approval

WarningRollback drops in-flight batches. Announce in #platform before step 3.

Steps

  1. Freeze the importer scheduler: kubectl -n importer scale deploy scheduler --replicas=0
  2. Drain the queue and record the lag snapshot.
  3. Flip traffic to the legacy path: kubectl set env deploy importer USE_LEGACY=true
  4. Verify vendor drops land in the ledger (see Verification).
  5. Unfreeze the scheduler and reopen the queue.

Verification

  • Ledger row counts match the vendor drop for the last hour
  • Dead-letter depth is flat for ten minutes
  • No new pages for fifteen minutes
